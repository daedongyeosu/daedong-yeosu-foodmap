7 Yeosu Breakfast Restaurants by Travel Route

A route-first guide covering Yeosu Expo Station, Yi Sun-sin Square, the ferry terminal, Singi-dong, Yeocheon and the airport-bound route.

By Daedong Yeosu Food MapInformation checked: August 23, 2026Not sponsored

Breakfast can be surprisingly difficult to plan in Yeosu, especially after an early train arrival or before a ferry departure. This guide compares data collected by Daedong Yeosu Food Map with publicly listed restaurant information and groups seven options by the route a traveler is likely to take.

Dine-in hours and delivery-app ordering hours are not the same.
Hours, prices and closing days can change. Check the latest map or ordering-app listing on the day you visit. If a train or ferry makes timing critical, call the restaurant first.

1. Early train arrival: Naejoguk Gukbap, Gwanmun

Located on the way from Yeosu Expo Station into central Yeosu, it is practical before an Expo or Odongdo itinerary.

  • Address: 84-3 Dongmun-ro, Yeosu
  • Phone: 061-665-5251
  • Dine-in listing: 24 hours, daily
  • Collected delivery-app hours: 07:00–01:00 next day
Check current map information ↗

2. Walking around Yi Sun-sin Square: Yeosu Gwangjang Gukbap

Convenient for Yi Sun-sin Square, Jinnamgwan and the Jungang-dong food streets.

  • Address: 3 Tongjeyeong 5-gil, Yeosu
  • Phone: 061-665-7333
  • Listed hours: 07:00–21:00; break 15:00–16:00
Check current map information ↗

3. Before an island ferry: Ara Sikdang

Near Yeosu Coastal Ferry Terminal and Gyodong Market, with regional dishes such as eel soup and crab set meals.

  • Address: 14-8 Yeogaekseonteomineol-gil, Yeosu
  • Phone: 061-642-9490
  • Listed hours: 07:00–21:00
Check current map information ↗

4. A home-style meal: Uleomni Sikdang

A useful alternative to soup-only breakfasts for travelers staying near Singi-dong or Yeosu City Hall.

  • Address: 75 Mangma-ro, Yeosu
  • Phone: 061-682-5982
  • Dine-in listing: 07:00–21:00
Check current map information ↗

5. Near City Hall: Kongsiru Main Branch

Mild and spicy bean-sprout soup options make this an approachable family breakfast.

  • Address: 85-1 Mangma-ro, Yeosu
  • Phone: 0507-1426-6026
  • Dine-in listing: Tue–Sat 24 hours; Monday from 06:30; Sunday until 21:00
  • Collected delivery-app hours: 06:30–15:00 and 17:00–20:20
Check current map information ↗

6. A 24-hour option in Yeocheon: Yangdaegam Galbitang

Convenient for hotels west of City Hall and travelers who prefer beef short-rib or ox-bone soup.

  • Address: 125 Hwasan-ro, Yeosu
  • Phone: 061-682-8788
  • Dine-in listing: 24 hours, daily
  • Collected delivery-app hours: 07:00–20:00
Check current map information ↗

7. Heading toward Suncheon or the airport: Il-deung Jin Gukbap

Its Jukrim location works well on the route out of central Yeosu toward Suncheon or Yeosu Airport.

  • Address: 13-36 Jukrimjungang-ro, Sora-myeon, Yeosu
  • Phone: 061-681-9933
  • Listed hours: Mon–Sat 07:00–21:00; closed Sunday

How this guide was checked

We used structured restaurant and ordering-app data collected for Daedong Yeosu Food Map to identify early-opening candidates, then compared addresses, phone numbers and dine-in hours with public map, restaurant-channel and Yeosu municipal information. Delivery-app hours are shown separately rather than presented as restaurant opening hours.

Prices and operating information reflect public listings checked on August 23, 2026 and may change.
